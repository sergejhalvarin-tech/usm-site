# Prototype Instructions

Run the local server yourself and open the preview in the browser available to this environment. Do not give the user server-start instructions when you can run it.

Before making substantial visual changes, use the Product Design plugin's `get-context` skill when the visual source is unclear or no longer matches the current goal. When the user gives durable prototype-specific design feedback, preferences, or decisions, record them in `AGENTS.md`.

When implementing from a selected generated mock, treat that image as the source of truth for layout, component anatomy, density, spacing, color, typography, visible content, and hierarchy.

Build app UI in `src/`. Keep `.openai/hosting.json`, `worker/index.js`, `scripts/prepare-sites-build.mjs`, and `tests/sites-worker.test.mjs` intact so the same local prototype can be handed to Sites. Before a Sites handoff, run `npm run build` and `npm run test:sites`; the build must leave `dist/client/index.html`, `dist/server/index.js`, and `dist/.openai/hosting.json`.

## Design direction (2026-11-12, restart — supersedes the navy version)

Owner asked for a maximally simple, professional site for the brand (renamed PROMETHEY → USM on 2026-11-12). Warm light canvas `#f7f5f1`, graphite text `#15171b`, one accent — the flame gradient of the owner's logo (`#ff9a2a → #ff6a1a → #e3341c`), used sparingly (numbers, units, highlights). One typeface (Manrope) + JetBrains Mono for small labels; no serif. 3D stages sit on dark graphite cards `#16191e`. Sections: hero (render `public/assets/kiln_hero.webp`) → 01 Печь и оборудование (`src/KilnExplorer.jsx`) → 02 Видимые детали (`src/ProcessViz.jsx` + `src/process.js`) → 03 Характеристики → 04 Как мы работаем → 05 География поставок (`src/DeliveryMap.jsx`) → contact form. The former retort-loading/forklift section was removed at the owner's request — do not bring it back.

Kiln model: `public/assets/models/kiln_v5.glb`, built by `Furnace_Model/build_kiln_v5.py` from the drawing-based v4 model (УП040.003-4) plus the automation/safety package (objects named `АСУ_*`, `Безоп_*`). The explorer finds equipment by mesh-name regex (`src/data.js` → `equipment`); internal parts switch the cutaway on (`userData.cutaway_hide`). Blender truncates long Cyrillic names (63 bytes) — match by prefix.

Mascot «Огонёк» (`src/Mascot.jsx`, drawing in `src/Brand.jsx`) is the brand character: it lives on a layer with z-index 1 below headings/stages (z-index 2), runs along `[data-perch]` text lines, dips behind letters, hides behind `[data-hide]` (hero render, 3D stages) and shows section tips (`tips` in `src/data.js`). Keep new headings `data-perch` so it can use them.

## Hero, paint and picker (2026-11-12, owner approved the light site and asked for more)

- Hero is a dark presentation stage (`#0e1013`) in two columns: text · kiln render (`public/assets/kiln_hero.webp`, low-angle Cycles render with a transparent background). The rest of the site stays light.
- Industrial kiln paint (in `Furnace_Model/build_kiln_v5.py`, so it is baked into blend/glb): body graphite RAL 7021, loading doors flame orange of the mascot (`#ff7417`, owner request — replaced signal red), frames/profiles/locks black. Keep to these 2–3 colours.
- Header has two equal flame buttons: «Подобрать оборудование» opens the picker popup, «Получить расчёт» opens «Куда отправить предложение?» (owner request: no picker card in the hero). Both popups live in `src/Picker.jsx` (shared `Modal` with focus trap; `useOffer().open()` / `openPicker()`); the picker's button passes its choices to the offer dialog. The form is a demo — nothing is sent yet.
- Mascot on floating panels: a panel marked `data-float` calls `focusMascot(el, tip)` / `releaseMascot()` (window events `ogonek:focus` / `ogonek:release`); the mascot layer rises to z-index 60 while it is on the panel. Card tops marked `data-edge` are also perches.
- Sections 01 and 02 place the kiln inside a procedural production hall (`src/workshop.js`, modelled on the owner's warehouse photo): white sandwich-panel walls, steel columns and dark roof trusses, high-bay lamps, polished concrete, A2/B3/C1 zone signs (no yellow bollards/column guards — owner removed them), yellow floor marking around the kiln. The camera is clamped to stay inside (maxDistance 16, minPolarAngle 0.9).
- Key figures: 26–42 t of charcoal per month per kiln, commissioning and training 7–14 days (owner, 2026-11-12).
- Section 01 shows a clean model — no numbered pins on the 3D view. Picking an item in «Конструкция» flies the camera along an eased orbital arc to frame that part (distance from its bounding sphere, stays under the lamps), then slowly orbits it.
- Section 02 is driven by a real numerical model in `src/process.js` (simulated once, then played back): 6-layer wood pile with conduction + radiation, drying front, 3-component Arrhenius kinetics (hemicellulose/cellulose/lignin), afterburner with O₂ control λ = 21/(21−O₂), zone ≤ 950 °C with excess gas flared at the vent, pipe ТП200 pressure loss, chimney draft, fan affinity laws, fixed-carbon growth, Newton cooling. Logs in the cut retort are coloured per layer; hot steel glows with black-body colour. Keep it physics-based when changing it.
- Service access (owner review, 2026-11-12, «вариант 1»): the fan, motor and VFD stand at the back wall at the start of the DN200 air main; the ladder end of the kiln stays clear (0.8 m zone). Ladder: 0.6 m clear width, 300 mm rungs, no cage hoops (not needed below 5 m), stringers 1.1 m above the roof with a gooseneck; roof guardrail 1.1 m with a self-closing gate and a fall-arrest anchor. All of it is built in the `service access` block of `Furnace_Model/build_kiln_v5.py`; equipment item `access` in `src/data.js`.
- Fan package (owner approved): check valve + flexible insert on the outlet, suction mesh, coupling guard, rain hood, lockable repair switch at the motor; the VFD lives in a ventilated section next to the control cabinet (not on the hot wall). Section 02 has a «Смоделировать отказ вентилятора» interlock demo (air dampers + check valve closed, firebox blocked, vent open). Fan shaft power in `src/process.js` is 0.74 kW at 50 Hz (motor 1.5 kW) — keep it consistent with the model.
